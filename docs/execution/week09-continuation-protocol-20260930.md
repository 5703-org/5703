## Product and preserved invariants

The continuous learner path is textbook reading, explanation, progressive tutoring, practice feedback, revision and inspectable learning records. The eight product areas are: (1) released-book and chapter reader, reading position and scoped passage questions; (2) goals and concept prerequisites; (3) server-controlled choice, short explanation, numeric/unit and step practice; (4) progress-aware tutor workspace; (5) error book and revision queue; (6) source- and condition-aware memory centre; (7) notes and cited bookmarks; (8) administrator health, request-stage and quality centre. Each requires user-facing, API, persistence, failure and permission checks.

Ordinary textbook questions request a complete explanation by default. Hints are an explicit teaching flow; full explanation remains an explicit action inside it. Four official OpenStax books, 10,594 released E5 vectors, exact source pages, saved answers, source visibility and previous experiment conditions retain their identities. E0 remains no retrieval; E1 remains basic dense retrieval. Client DTOs exclude answer keys, protected drafts, other learners' records and raw provider secrets. Source selection is frozen at submission and enforced before retrieval, prior-evidence admission and publication.

The answer chain keeps question intent and conditions, evidence relevance, context sufficiency and claim support as separate recorded decisions. A named missing requirement permits one bounded targeted lookup against the same release. Generation, checking, planning and repair share one finite call and active-time budget. Final text and displayed sources publish atomically only after authentic-source, semantic-support and teaching-disclosure checks. Development may add versioned policies; old stored requests and studies retain their executable policy identity.

## Seven formal study families

These planning scales come from the user brief. They are not completed samples or a power claim. Development pilots determine and record final sizes, quality gates and precision targets before reserved execution.

Cases are grouped by knowledge concept across all four books. Official anchors, necessary points, conditions and unsupported conclusions are recorded before answer execution. Existing human labels are distinguished from program-derived and AI-generated labels. Independent semantic judges see blinded answer order and a common rubric; online checker decisions are not labels. Judge calibration includes order, length, style and injected-instruction sensitivity. A second model-family adapter is reserved, with actual use reported only if available. Simulated students receive only learner-visible output; persona consistency, hidden-answer ignorance and guidance response are checked before tutor results are interpreted. Human learning gain, classroom accessibility and independent human ratings require their own observations.

## Metrics and uncertainty

All scheduled requests retain terminal records. Reports split substantive answers/hints, programmed evidence refusals, clarification, model or local-execution failure, timeout, empty output and invalid evaluation. The teaching endpoint is useful, accurate, correctly cited hints within current and cumulative help allowance divided by all planned hint requests. Other denominators include supported-question correctness, wrong refusal, unsupported answer, required-point coverage, claim citation support, checker false block/release, memory true/false/omitted selection and safe legitimate-task completion. Semantic metrics name their label source. Unscored and disputed cases remain unscored.

Use paired comparisons by concept/task, grouped confidence intervals, explicit repeat observations and correction for multiple secondary comparisons. Report B minus A, C minus A, D minus A and interaction D minus B minus C plus A; report the external release separately. All, successful and failed latency distributions remain separate. Count submitted calls and observed input/output/cache tokens for every outcome, then estimate cost from the tariff valid at execution. Retain unknown counters and distinguish estimates from invoices. Do not promote the previous study's 160 answers, ten refusals and thirty failures into new experimental outcomes.
