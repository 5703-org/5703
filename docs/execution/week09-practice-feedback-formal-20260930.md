A read-only API source audit checked all fourteen source units against the active release before freezing. A separate read-only SQL extraction checked the full unit text and page for reviewer materials. Original-PDF hashes are present in the receipt.

The reserved outcome distribution was twelve `correct`, eleven `incorrect` and one `insufficient`. All ten reserved items reached the completed state and produced a review entry after a non-correct submission. The measured latency covers only the learner attempt POST. It excludes source reading, item authoring, validation, publication and time taken by the learner. Practice grading used `deterministic_rules_v2` and made **zero answer-model provider calls**, so observed provider cost for this study was USD 0. The rest of the project's DeepSeek QA and teaching execution belongs to separate study families.

One deliberately included source-grounded challenge is a response stating that a eukaryotic cell has **no membrane-bound nucleus** while retaining organelles. The system returned `insufficient` with `ambiguous_negation`, kept the nucleus point missing and did not mark the response correct. The rule result matches the preregistered contract. Reviewers must judge whether the explicit misconception deserves an `incorrect` outcome and whether the feedback gives enough correction. This case illustrates why rule-concordance cannot be reported as semantic accuracy.

## Human review and limits

Two [blank independent review forms](../../evidence/week09-continuation/20260930/practice-feedback-review/reviewer-A-blank.csv) and [the second form](../../evidence/week09-continuation/20260930/practice-feedback-review/reviewer-B-blank.csv) contain all 24 reserved attempts in separately randomized orders. Each row includes the exact released source-unit text, official URL and physical page, item, submitted response and visible feedback. The [scoring guide](../../evidence/week09-continuation/20260930/practice-feedback-review/SCORING_GUIDE.md) asks reviewers to assess solvability, response correctness, feedback accuracy and actionability. The coordinator-only mapping and automatic rule expectations stay outside these forms. [`practice_feedback_review.py`](../../evaluation/week09_continuation/practice_feedback_review.py) rejects edited source/question/feedback text, unknown or duplicate IDs, partial scores, missing reasons and invalid timestamps during import. **Completed independent ratings: 0/24 per reviewer.**

The catalogue was authored and source-checked by the development process. The wrong/correct attempt pairs exercise grading transitions and revision records; they do not measure learner improvement. The ten reserved items are below the approximately 160-question planning scale and were selected from a small set of textbook concepts, so the observations do not estimate population-level educational quality. This registered family currently has a candidate arm only. A previous-release practice comparator was unavailable, and no previous-versus-candidate effect is claimed. The general Week 9 QA and teaching arms retain their separate frozen identities.

The reusable [execution runner](../../evaluation/week09_continuation/practice_feedback_formal.py), [integrity analysis](../../evaluation/week09_continuation/practice_feedback_analysis.py) and [focused tests](../../tests/unit/test_week09_practice_feedback.py) are part of the current source. Focused validation passed 2/2 tests, Ruff lint and formatting. The full integrated software gate for the successor source is recorded separately by the release owner. The next formal expansion should diversify authors and concepts, include independent pre-run solvability labels, add equivalence and misconception probes for short explanations, and collect two independent human feedback judgments before drawing educational conclusions.
