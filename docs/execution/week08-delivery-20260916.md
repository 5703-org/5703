## Implemented shared flow

New interactive requests freeze `conversation_preparer_v9`, `question_understanding_v2`, `interactive_evidence_union_v1`, `interactive_relevance_v1`, optional `interactive_facet_fallback_v1`, query device and answer mode. The structured record retains original/standalone questions, intent, topic relation, explicit clause facets, comparison operands, numbers, negation, conditions, correction provenance and clarification reason. It is bounded deterministic English syntax; no hidden understanding-model call or semantic-equivalence guarantee is claimed. Explicit v6/v7/v8 commands keep their recorded behavior.

A unique correction such as “I mean RNA, not DNA” preserves the prior comparison or condition. Collapsing both comparison operands or leaving an action unresolved requires clarification. A current “this topic/comparison” can refer to a complete named comparison; a bare ambiguous “it” does not choose an operand. A complete problem with a named subject performing a predicate retains its possessive reference. An extracted interrogative-only antecedent such as “What” falls back to the full prior learner question, preserving its action and conditions. Bare RAG remains ambiguous until learner context supplies its meaning; biological RAG1/ragweed retain their domain.

Expanding follow-ups validate prior citations against the request's pinned release, current document visibility and exact processing/text identity. Fresh and eligible prior passages form a deduplicated union capped at 20, including at most five inherited admissions; all receive current-question scoring. Reuse-only requests with unavailable sources do not substitute new sources as the old evidence. Learned scores remain tied to the fixed model/revision. An explicitly unconfigured union uses declared BM25, without silently switching after a configured reranker failure.

The provisional -4.0 MiniLM raw-logit threshold is unchanged and tied to revision `233902d25c440f23af6f7d6e94d2946bac0bee0a`. Its original five-positive/three-negative development and eight-holdout diagnostics are not independent calibration. Scores are neither probabilities nor semantic support labels. The calibration CLI binds the declared complete pool and requires real reviewed judgments; blank/incomplete/tampered judgments fail and a proposed threshold never activates automatically.

When the whole-question screen accepts no candidate and at least two explicit meaningful facets exist, the frozen fallback independently retrieves/reranks at most two facets. It shares **20 additional candidate-scoring slots total**, normally ten each, after the original whole-query screen of at most 20. These are separate recorded stages, so total work may reach 40 ranking pairs. Each extra local stage checks the same persisted active-time budget and cancellation state. Final admitted generation candidates remain at most 20, with no comparison of scores from different facet queries. The full original question and every requested facet remain in generation; missing parts are not silently removed. `facet_fallback` records per-query/model/revision scores and exclusions, and `final_selection` records distinct screened/admitted IDs/counts rather than sums across duplicate appearances.

Interactive generation deduplicates whole passages and selects complementary facets/qualifications within the unchanged 3,000-token evidence budget and complete model-window accounting. `lexical_facets_v1` reports full/partial/none lexical coverage and missing terms; synonyms and entailment remain limits. Structural citations are enforced, while support/completeness review flags retain null independent ratings. Teaching plans connect explicit level/style, requested hints, conditions and misconception handling; hint compact-answer leakage uses the existing one-repair budget. Conflict handling is an evidence-conditioned instruction, not a validated contradiction detector.

Textbook mode is default. Empty or all-rejected evidence produces a grounded citation-free limitation without a provider call. Explicit `general_knowledge` is a separate per-request choice with `model_general_knowledge_unverified` provenance: no textbook retrieval, evidence or citations, and no automatic switch after refusal/error. Mock general knowledge explicitly refuses independent factual generation. Retry and regeneration retain the chosen mode; old textbook idempotency hashes remain compatible. Failed regeneration retains the old answer. Formal E0/E1 adopts none of these interactive policies.

The administrator allowlist projection exposes original/rewritten question, requirements, reuse/fallback decisions, separate counts, model/corpus/device, timings and terminal errors. It excludes secrets, arbitrary trace/configuration dumps and raw provider responses. Missing historical fields stay null. The learner sees mode/provenance and citation limits.
