## Controlled design

Three original public textbook concepts have frozen negative and positive synthetic answers. Both A (existing) and B (opt-in textbook_context_qualification_v1) receive the same complete question, answer and original source bank. S01/S02/S03 retain 64/54/50 original blocks, with original version and slice binding. The source-hash preparation mistake was corrected by distinguishing full-chunk and selected-slice hashes; no corpus replacement occurred. Source versions, questions, draft/control labels and original-to-actual packet alias joins are frozen before dispatch.

The new S01 negative explicitly says no change at any stage of catalysis. Its wording inherits the textbook summary but adds an unsupported all-stage scope, so it is not labeled fully literal-faithful. The positive distinguishes net/overall persistence from temporary shape and covalent-binding changes. S02 contrasts stopping all water molecules with ongoing bidirectional movement at zero net flux. S03 contrasts an unsupported universal Kc extension with the stated constant-temperature scope; the source is not claimed to establish a direct temperature relationship. All six controls passed targeted independent label/citation review. They are diagnostic labels, not formal human-scored benchmark gold.

Normal compiler/local-gate replay uses legacy_context_v1 identically for A/B to retain the already frozen full evidence bank, rather than reselecting excerpts. Only checker policy/schema/instruction differ. No new answer generation is measured. All twelve candidate payloads were serialized before dispatch. Full protective holds sum to CNY2.375956; the controller only admits a complete A/B pair when settled known cost plus retained UNKNOWN plus that pair's full protective reservation fits CNY1.00. It releases only the newly settled hold and defers whole pairs when capacity is insufficient. Request order was balanced A/B then B/A across controls. It admits at most twelve physical checker attempts, with fixed model, nonthinking, temperature0, JSON object and output4096, no retries, fallback, redirects or environment proxy.
