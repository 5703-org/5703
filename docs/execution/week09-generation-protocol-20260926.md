## Objective and fixed behavior

Increase delivery of supported and useful answers while reducing unnecessary checker repairs. Ordinary textbook questions retain complete answers by default. Explicit hints use progress-aware teaching controls. Textbook claims need authentic, currently visible, semantically supportive citations. General knowledge has its separate basis and no textbook certification. Teaching disclosure, cumulative exposure, owner isolation, memory deletion and source revocation remain publication conditions. Original E0 and E1 retain their definitions. Remote provider calls remain bounded by four calls and 180 active seconds per request; supplementary retrieval is bounded and measured separately within the overall request deadline.

## Common engineering changes

Introduce a new version of the generation/checking contract, retain prior executable policy identities, and validate the actual draft citation bindings instead of requesting a fresh preferred source set. Claim variants carry only applicable fields. Repairs target identified defects and preserve unaffected content. The final exact response, visible sources and teaching limits are rechecked after every alteration. Provider schema probes test actual generation/checking structures separately from connectivity. Empty responses and invalid contracts retain raw protected receipts and counted bounded recovery attempts.

Evidence coverage records distinguish the available context from the draft's use of it. A missing locatable requirement may trigger at most one targeted retrieval pass. Coverage heuristics are recorded as automatic estimates and never replace semantic validation or independent source-grounded labels.

## Development, pilot and reserved groups

Previously observed failure questions and all inspected old outputs are development material. New pilot and reserved cases are grouped by knowledge concept, with all paraphrases, conversation turns and variants of a concept confined to one partition. Catalogue hashes, expected actions and official reference anchors are frozen before provider execution. Failures remain scheduled outcomes; infrastructure interruption, empty content and semantic refusal have distinct labels.

First run a small new-topic pilot with repeated paired conditions. Use its observed paired differences, discordance and latency variation to document the proposed formal sample size before releasing reserved cases. Automatic operational measures can guide an engineering sample-size estimate; they cannot supply missing independent human quality labels. If the available groups or unfilled ratings cannot establish the planned precision, report that limitation and preserve the reserved groups and formal recommendation. Do not repeatedly inspect a held-out set while tuning.

## Outcomes and review

Record context sufficiency, draft support and delivery as separate fields. Primary human teaching outcome is a useful, correctly supported hint within the current and cumulative disclosure allowance. Report delivery per scheduled request; first-check acceptance; checker false blocks and false releases against independent labels; citation support and completeness; required-point coverage; repair count and preserved content; empty/schema/timeout failures; calls and input/output/cache tokens; estimated cost; all/success/failure latency distributions. Report automatic and human results separately. No human scores are generated by the implementation.

Create blinded materials for accepted and rejected drafts, including source anchors and the exact draft judged. Two reviewers rate independently; disagreements remain visible and adjudication is separate. Provide scoring instructions, a validated importer and explicit denominators. Automated quality proxies and unlabelled cases must remain identifiable. Learning gains and delayed transfer need a separate study.
