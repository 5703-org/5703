# Eight pilot visual source reviews — 3 October 2026

Two fresh-context Codex reviewers inspected eight original physical PDF pages:
four figure candidates and four formula candidates. They viewed 24 full-page,
exact-region and surrounding-context images. Root verified all retained image
hashes and sixteen exact catalog records, including eight unique V2-to-V4
lineages. Four official PDF identities remain unchanged. These observations extend
the [full V4 mechanical run](week09-visual-v4-full-validation-20261003.md).

All eight selected regions correspond to their actual originals. Their V4 text
and geometry preserve the selected V2 representations. None of the four figure
representations encodes the complete selected graphic. Among the four formula
candidates, two are incidental prose glosses, one is an incomplete arithmetic
expression, and one is a complete narrow value example. These selected cases
supply no general-formula extraction accuracy estimate.

The original PDFs retain the complete visible diagrams and surrounding text.
The findings concern extracted candidate representations. Original-page viewing,
retrieval, generated answers and downstream question correctness were not tested
by these reviews. Scientific currency was not independently assessed. Content
recovery requires a separately versioned producer and actual consumer checks;
these reviews performed no catalog repair or publication.

Both reviewers used the same AI model family and exposed development cases.
Initial figure-review console output included reserved metadata; no reserved
original page was opened or adjudicated. The formula reviewer remained limited
to the four pilot cases. The original 120-row review sheet retains all 600 blank
judgment fields. Human ratings, formal labels, administrative decisions,
publication approvals and downstream QA executions remain zero for this work.
