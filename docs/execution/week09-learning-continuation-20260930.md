## Product workflow and implementation

The reader opens an authorized released book, preserves its physical-page and processing identity, resumes a versioned reading position and sends an exact Unicode selection through the existing chat service. Chapter, textbook and all-workspace scope are enforced before retrieval, fallback and inherited evidence; the same identity is rechecked before publication. The original-page endpoint renders the hash-pinned stored PDF through authentication. Original source bytes, active released vectors and historical citations remain separate from new visual candidates.

A goal selects actual sections and learning depth. Its units distinguish reading, practice and review; section-order prerequisites are labelled suggestions. Publisher-labelled objectives are extracted conservatively when the words remain in the cleaned source. A semantic prerequisite graph, confusion relation or mastered percentage is not invented from a heading or a single answer.

Practice supports single choice, multiple choice, short explanation, numeric/unit and step tasks: five wire kinds within the four pedagogical families in the brief. Private rubrics, acceptable expressions, conditions, common errors and hints remain server-side. Numeric checking uses an explicit finite unit table and saved tolerance, with no arbitrary expression execution. Short-answer rule matches retain null independent semantic verification. Attempts preserve the exact item revision, response, feedback, progress version and idempotency receipt.

Hint and full-explanation actions are explicit disclosures. Correct current-step work may advance a practice task; requesting help alone does not. V5 chat tutoring additionally uses the original problem, recent actually delivered feedback and current learner attempt. It distinguishes unrelated work without progressing the task. The online checker is an automatic operational gate, not an independent scientific or pedagogical judge.

Review records are based on actual attempts, not reading clicks. Transparent intervals and recent-three error counts support inspectable recommendations and related-question selection. Optional practice memories identify their own item and attempt, retain the grading method and do not establish general mastery. Existing opt-in, edit, expiry, deletion and source constraints continue to apply. Personal notes/bookmarks and review cards are separately labelled and can be exported with available source provenance; revocation does not silently restore a source link.

Administrator question generation, validation and publication are distinct. A model proposal uses one finite call against the exact requested source, records its pre-call identity durably and creates an unpublished draft. It has no automatic retry or publication. Structural/source validation records issue codes; publication requires the current revision and an explicit review confirmation. The generated key, worked explanation and unreviewed draft do not enter learner item views or the textbook index.

The actual lost-receipt browser check committed the first step, discarded its HTTP receipt, reloaded the advanced state and replayed the exact original body/key/version. Three POSTs produced two saved attempts, preserving the first receipt and avoiding an extra step. The browser also verified source-key omission, reading selection, review rescheduling, note exports, practice-memory provenance and manual draft validation/publication. These software observations do not certify question solvability or learning gain.

Detailed boundaries are in the [backend record](learning-workspace-backend-20260930.md), [V5 answer-core record](week09-answer-core-20260930.md), [data model](../foundation/data_model.md), [API contract](../foundation/api_contract.md) and [architecture](../foundation/architecture.md).
