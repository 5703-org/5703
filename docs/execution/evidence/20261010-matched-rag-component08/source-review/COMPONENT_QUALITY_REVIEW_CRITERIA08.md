# Predeclared development comparison review criteria08

Prepared before any component08 paid generation. The shared question is `What is homeostasis?`. This is one exposed development case with two retrieval arms, not a held-out accuracy benchmark or human Gold set.

For each scheduled arm, retain the actual request, provider raw response, final outcome and failed attempts. An unrun, failed or unsupported arm remains in the two-arm denominator. A successful provider/schema/checker gate is reported separately from independent source assessment.

The required question-level content is a clear definition of homeostasis as maintaining relatively stable internal conditions. Dynamic regulation, negative feedback and physiological examples are optional extensions for this short question; if present, their substantive predicates must also be supported. Do not add rubric requirements after seeing an answer to manufacture a winner. Record whether the answer misleadingly describes absolute constancy rather than relative stability.

Independent source assessment reads the actually generated claims and the exact source fragments selected by the actual checker. Assess every predicate of a compound claim against that selected fragment union. Full paragraph availability, a declared support status, exact quote matching alone, or a separate unselected source does not establish entailment. Preserve separate original fragments and layout limits. Report complete, partial, unsupported and unassessed claim counts with the actual claim denominator, plus the concrete reason for every shortfall. Report optional details as optional; do not treat a longer answer as automatically better.

Record source provenance and whether source text was actually supplied to the checker. Distinguish all five frozen retrieval items from any subsequent teacher context packing or truncation. Check raw provider fields separately from lawful ordinary-question conditional normalization; never claim that a provider emitted a supplemented `not_applicable` field.

Compare generation component elapsed times from the actual start of `GenerationService.generate` to its final return. Teacher and checker latencies may be shown separately. Prior retrieval preparation was sequential and includes a cold/warm difference, so those observations cannot identify a faster retrieval algorithm. Do not sum disconnected phase timings into a measured browser request. Native Send-to-complete-DOM timing remains null unless actually observed in the authorized native workflow.

Retain all costs and UNKNOWN dispositions. Do not promote the default RAG policy, change registry statuses, infer p95, claim learning benefit or mark the whole project complete from this pair.
