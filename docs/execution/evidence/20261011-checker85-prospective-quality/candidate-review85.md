Optional deeper mechanics were not promoted into new question requirements.

## Smallest proposed change

Generation explicitly retains the source-restricted object and counting basis, including a necessary antecedent, and cites all actual complementary evidence locally. It may narrow an optional unsupported addition or name a genuine requested gap. Existing learner-given and derivation rules remain; a calculated answer need not occur verbatim in a source.

Before marking a unit supported, the checker evaluates the draft's actual wording against that unit's own returned supporting IDs. Another unit's correct relationship, mere entity presence or a corrected qualifier in the reason cannot substitute for proof. All necessary source premises must be explicitly selected for that unit and its own parent citation. Meaning-preserving paraphrases and inferences forced by the selected combined premises remain allowed. Negative units retain their actual contradictory/irrelevant selections and honest flags. An optional draft defect does not add a missing user requirement.

Existing general quantity, referent, role/stage and joint selection rules are already present. This change makes two observed failure modes operational at the final unit decision and generation sentence, without repeating their whole rules or tightening reason caps. It does not establish that these model instructions will be obeyed. No safe general deterministic textual gate was identified for the semantic omissions: keyword overlap cannot prove a relationship or legitimate inference, and a category heuristic could reject valid source-grounded explanations.
