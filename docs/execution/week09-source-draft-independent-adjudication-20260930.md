# Week 9 source and rejected-draft adjudication pilot

The evaluator-only `week09_source_draft_adjudication_v1` protocol freezes actual cited OpenStax excerpts from three already exposed historical draft-review packets. The packets come from the previously verified [external reviewer inventory](../../../deliverables/CS30-1_Week09_Reviewer_Materials_20260930/MANIFEST.json). One packet requests a complete textbook answer and two request a first teaching hint. Across the three packets there are five cited excerpts from *Anatomy and Physiology 2e* and *Biology 2e*. These cases are development material; later reserved comparisons must exclude their knowledge concepts.

The [selection receipt](../../evidence/week09-continuation/20260930/source-draft-adjudication-selection-v1-tariff-frozen.json) binds the exact historical inventory, three packet hashes, evaluator source, prompt and response schema. The model-facing cases hide the original packet IDs, historical outcome, online checker decision and coordinator mapping. The exact question, draft and passage text stay in `E:/5703/week09-private-db/source-draft-adjudication-v1-attempt5-20260930/`. Each passage carries its official OpenStax URL, section, physical PDF page and SHA-256 of the excerpt shown to the evaluator. The original packet inventory supplies byte-level input provenance; this pilot does not independently rerun PDF transcription or reconstruct the complete generation prompt.

The evaluator distinguishes (1) whether the **actually cited excerpts** can support useful help at the requested level, (2) whether they can support a complete answer, and (3) whether the existing draft is factually correct, supported by its citations, complete for the current request and within the permitted help level. A draft can fail even when its cited textbook passage contains the answer. Each positive or partial sufficiency opinion must quote an exact substring of one supplied passage. The versioned validator rejects unknown source IDs, altered quotes, duplicate issue types, contradictory publishability fields and missing explanations. Raw provider outputs, if later authorized, receive durable pre-call reservations and remain private. Published receipts contain counts, hashes, state, token use and an estimated cost only. The run command requires an explicit peak/off-peak choice matched to the [official DeepSeek schedule](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/), including its public-holiday rule; invoice reconciliation stays separate.

The [offline preflight](../../evidence/week09-continuation/20260930/source-draft-adjudication-preflight-v1-tariff-frozen.json) completed **3/3** frozen packet checks. It verified all five cited excerpts, their text hashes, official asset hosts, page fields and the citation IDs used in each draft. It generated two independently ordered blank review sheets. A separate [read-only parser scan](../../evidence/week09-continuation/20260930/source-draft-adjudication-parser-scan-v1.json) covered all 24 retained historical draft packets and their 74 cited passages. Four focused tests, Ruff lint and format, and direct mypy checking passed. Preflight used **zero model calls and zero semantic labels**. The original reviewer materials, answers, citations, corpus and database were not changed.

An earlier, separately frozen [eight-case DeepSeek source-only development pilot](../../evidence/week09-learning/20260930/source-sufficiency-ai-pilot-v1.json) remains available for context. It rated accepted retrieval excerpts on 20 authored required points: 14 supported, three partial, two unsupported and one uncertain. Its eight overall AI labels were four sufficient, two partial, one insufficient and one uncertain. It used eight provider calls and estimated USD 0.003618414 from the recorded tariff, with zero human ratings. That study judged **accepted** excerpts and authored points; the new three-case protocol judges **actually cited** excerpts and existing drafts. Their denominators and source scopes are separate.

The attempted external DeepSeek run for the new three-case pilot was rejected by automatic approval review before process launch. The [blocked-run receipt](../../evidence/week09-continuation/20260930/source-draft-adjudication-run-block-v1.json) records **zero submitted calls, zero new AI ratings and zero new cost**. The review cited the disclosure of these specific internal draft packets and project context to DeepSeek without explicit payload-level authorization and instructed against indirect execution. The frozen cases and offline review sheets remain ready for a permitted run. No raw online checker verdict is available for these packets, so the current evidence cannot calculate a checker false-block or false-release rate. Actual generation-context sufficiency also remains unknown because these packets contain cited excerpts only.

The executable is [`adjudication.py`](../../evaluation/week09_continuation/adjudication.py), with focused checks in [`test_week09_adjudication.py`](../../tests/unit/test_week09_adjudication.py). Any later AI ratings must remain labelled `ai_generated_same_family`, separate from online checker decisions and independent human ratings. This exposed development sample cannot serve as a fresh formal holdout after candidate integration and freeze.
