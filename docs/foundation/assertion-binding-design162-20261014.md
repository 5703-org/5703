# Generic assertion-to-fragment selection: bounded design

Design only. No product edit, implementation, test execution, GEN request or additional paid request is authorized by this note. The current phase162 grant belongs exclusively to its two CHECK-only observations; this proposal receives none of that budget. Current phase161 source and failed native records remain unchanged.

## Two boundaries

Fragment selection is a semantic proposal: the generator must choose the passages that establish a complete assertion. Lossless binding is mechanical: the compiler proves that the proposed identifiers refer to the actual submitted text, owner, hash and offsets. Correct binding can faithfully preserve a bad selection. A checker refusal can also be wrong even when the selected union is complete. Neither result permits the compiler to add proof or certify truth.

Prefer evidence-ID-first generation references: each whole assertion selects an existing evidence_id, then its permitted fragment_ids, from the request's immutable hierarchy. The server copies the exact selected blocks into its ledger and derives public markers. This reduces confusion between evidence and fragment numbering without making their names meaningful evidence. Do not encode a topic, figure number, desired answer or known DEV label in selection rules.

## Frozen contract and permissible revision

An assertion record binds the original request, answer surface, exact full prose and range, existing canonical claim ID, local row ID, inline citation set, and selected source union. A source record binds bank digest, evidence/fragment IDs, asset/document/processing/source-unit identity, exact text/hash, Unicode offsets, complete-block status and current release/visibility. Repeated wording requires its exact existing locator; text matching alone cannot select its owner.

For a fixed-body repair, allowed candidates are complete fragments already submitted under that assertion's actual cited evidence IDs. The entire submitted bank is an inventory, not a grant to borrow another assertion's proof. A new evidence ID requires a separately authorized controlled draft revision with changed citation markers and fresh checks; it cannot enter this fixed-body route. No retrieval, neighboring PDF lookup, passage enlargement or question-only proof is allowed.

A selection proposal names only eligible failed claim IDs, their prior-binding digest and one replacement union per target. It cannot change prose, order, surfaces, claim IDs, public evidence-ID membership, sources, model configuration or previously accepted selections. Source and assertion identities are recomputed before acceptance. The proposal contains no trusted support label: actor identity, conditions, quantities, polarity and causal relations remain independently judged from the complete union.

## Routing and state transitions

| Observation | Bounded next state |
| --- | --- |
| Unknown, foreign or duplicate ID; extra checker assertion; corrupt quote/offset/hash | CONTRACT_BLOCKED. Preserve raw output. Source reselection cannot repair checker metadata. |
| Correct binding, incomplete own selection, and a plausible missing premise in the same eligible submitted scope | SELECTION_PROPOSAL_ELIGIBLE, subject to explicit authority and remaining budget. |
| Complete own union with a disputed semantic refusal | CHECKER_DIAGNOSIS_ONLY. Do not keep adding fragments or seek repeated favorable votes. |
| Necessary actor/condition/bridge absent from the eligible scope | TRUE_SCOPE_GAP. Preserve the failure; a later content/citation revision needs separate authority. |
| New union passes mechanical checks | PROPOSAL_BOUND. It is not a supported answer. |
| One complete fresh checker accepts all original assertion, coverage and publication gates | VERIFIED_CANDIDATE for a separately integrated normal publication path. |
| Any proposal/check failure, uncertainty, stale source or exhausted budget | TERMINAL. No fallback, retry, second reselection or publication override. |

Routing needs an explicit saved diagnosis; a lexical overlap score or a model's negative reason alone is insufficient authority. An unchanged or already maximal selected union is not a new candidate. If uncertainty remains about whether the gap is selection or checker calibration, stop and report that uncertainty.

## Persistence, limits and controls

Preserve the original GEN output, bad binding, raw CHECK, native failure and fees. Append a candidate revision containing its exact parent/body/claim/bank/binding hashes, changed claim IDs, before/after unions, selector request/response, model and usage, mechanical validation, source visibility check, and fresh checker result. Never rewrite the old command, label the original draft supported, reopen a closed budget or replace a published answer before a new candidate passes the normal atomic publication transaction. Existing private draft/attempt storage may carry these records; a new database schema is not assumed.

Proposed future cap: one selection call and one complete fresh CHECK for one candidate, at most two targeted assertions, at most12 references per assertion, and the existing whole-bank/output/time limits. No transport, format, semantic or contract retries. These calls require separate explicit scope and cost fences and count against durable remaining allowances; the closed phase161 budget and current CHECK-only162 budget cannot fund them. No GEN-body rerun is part of the fixed-body experiment. An unpromoted experimental candidate has publication=false.

Meaningful offline controls should cover one complementary union with its actor bridge, removal of each required condition or bridge, a wrong actor or reversed relation, generic definition without instance evidence, same wording at different locators, foreign-parent references, changed hashes/spans/IDs, another assertion's proof, new evidence IDs, stale/withdrawn sources, and an already complete union wrongly routed to reselection. Also prove original failures stay immutable, unaffected content/bindings remain exact, one-candidate caps and UNKNOWN exposure stop execution, and an optimistic checker flag cannot bypass any gate. Authored labels validate mechanics and known counterexamples; they do not measure live entailment accuracy.

Primary risk remains semantic choice: hierarchical references and exact ledgers cannot force the generator or checker to understand a scientific relationship. A favorable result for two seen DEV questions would not establish general qualification. Preserve this distinction when assessing the separate CHECK-only observations.
